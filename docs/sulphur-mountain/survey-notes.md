### Where the county's parcel line disagrees with the record

The county GIS parcel polygon matches the record on the east (road) side but drifts **up to 29 ft west of the record line along the west side** and about 10 ft on the panhandle. The county draws the north line at about 657 ft; the record says 630.04. This is ordinary assessor-fabric error, not a survey dispute — but it means the county's line should not be used to site anything near the west boundary or the easement.

## What this means in practice

- **Note 2 matters for the loan.** This is a preliminary boundary, not a boundary establishment survey. A lender or the county can ask for monument recovery and a filed Record of Survey before anything is built near a line. Budget for it.
- **The house is on the record.** The county's building-footprint layer classes the three structures it maps here as field-and-seed-crops; the sheet shows a house, two buildings and five sheds. The assessor carries 1,277 sq ft of improvement. Reconcile this before an appraisal does.
- **The stamp says a permit set went in.** Building and Safety approved this sheet on 30 January 2026 as part of a plan set — there is a county permit file for this parcel that the platform does not yet pull.
